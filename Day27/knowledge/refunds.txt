Refund Eligibility

Enterprise customers may request a refund within 60 calendar days of purchase. Pro and Free customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of purchase.

Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, customers should receive the funds within 5–7 business days. Processing time may vary depending on the customer's bank or payment provider.

Non-Refundable Items

Promotional credits are not refundable. Credits issued as part of a promotion cannot be exchanged for cash or returned to the customer's original payment method.
